Mission, Architecture & Limitations

About DisasterGuard

A full-stack emergency response and crisis communications system demonstrating how digital tools enhance disaster resilience.

Purpose of the Platform

In catastrophic weather events and sudden civil crises, fragmented communication and delayed situational intelligence cost lives. DisasterGuard is created to unify four vital pillars of emergency response into a coherent, accessible interface:

Real-Time Threat Alerts

Centralized broadcasts detailing hazard severity, coordinates, and concrete safety directives.

Geospatial Intelligence

Interactive Leaflet maps depicting hazard zones, trauma hospitals, and evacuation hubs.

Citizen Incident Intake

Crowdsourced reports with GPS coordinates, photos, and unique reference codes for live tracking.

Shelter & Supply Logistics

Live bed capacity meters and amenities directories to channel displaced families smoothly.

Community & Volunteer Mobilization

Civil preparedness relies not merely on centralized authorities, but on educated neighborhoods and rapid community cooperation. DisasterGuard facilitates community mobilization by enabling:

  • Community volunteers to view unverified hazard reports and cross-verify conditions on the ground.
  • Households to maintain their digital 72-Hour Survival Go-Bag checklist without needing accounts.
  • Emergency logistics teams to monitor shelter capacity and prevent overcrowding at individual evacuation centers.

Academic Prototype Limitations & Disclaimer

Important Notice: DisasterGuard is an educational engineering prototype built for college project demonstration and software architectural evaluation.

  • Not an Official Warning Service: The alerts, incident tickets, and hospital bed counts displayed on this website are simulated demonstration records.
  • No Guaranteed Responder Dispatch: Submitting a report via this website logs a ticket in the local system; it does not automatically dispatch municipal rescue forces or fire departments.
  • Demonstration Mode Support: The system features a zero-configuration persistent storage mode that operates out-of-the-box without requiring an external MongoDB server, while seamlessly supporting full MongoDB clustering if configured.

In any actual real-world emergency situation, immediately contact your regional civil protection dispatchers at 112 or local emergency services.
