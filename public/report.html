Life-Threatening Emergency Notice

If you or people around you face an active life hazard, fire, or acute medical trauma, do not rely solely on online web submission forms. Call 112 (National Emergency) or 108 (Ambulance) immediately for instant emergency dispatch.

Reporting Guidelines

  • Provide exact street names, landmarks, or GPS coordinates to accelerate response.
  • Specify whether casualties or elderly residents require immediate physical assistance.
  • Do not submit speculative or false reports; emergency resources must remain unencumbered.
